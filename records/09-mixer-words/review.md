# Review of attempt-04, claim 1: thinner low-order tables, word stages, story-boundary weighting

Numbers come from verify.json, base.json, the incumbent's test.json and dev.json, attempt-03's logs, the agent's work/r logs, and my own short runs on this Mac (metered step prices, smoke tables at 2e9 ticks, 16 to 128 dev windows). No verify.py at the real budget.

1. Mechanism. The gain is real modelling, and most of it is at eval rather than in training. Pooled over 16 seeds it is 0.00933 +- 0.00068 bpb. Pricing real steps and walking them to the budget reproduces `train_ticks` exactly for both entries (39,965,835,468 and 39,974,488,916), so the step counts below are exact. A sampled table step costs 15,076,632 ticks against 18,351,898 (17.8% less), after 301 full-price steps. Table steps: incumbent 2003, attempt-03 2258, entry 2372. Bytes trained: 16,408,576, 18,497,536, 19,431,424, which is 446,421, 503,249 and 528,753 bytes per 1e9 ticks, so the entry trains 18.4% more bytes per tick and attempt-03 12.7% more. The orders 5 to 12 and the mixer get all of that; orders 1 to 3 go live on about 820 steps against 2003, order 4 on about 1340. The gate pays for the new stages: a batch costs 24,725,447 ticks against 18,593,481, so it gets 130 batches against 173 (133,120 bytes against 177,152).

Attribution is not clean, and only the total is verified. Attempt-03's similar schedule gained 0.00248 +- 0.00083 on the sealed split at +12.7% bytes. The agent's same schedule alone (`samp1`, one seed) scored 1.1066 on dev against the published 1.1082, a gain of 0.0016. I put the schedule at about 0.002 to 0.003, a quarter of the total or a little more, and it is the same effect as attempt-03. The remaining 0.0063 to 0.0073 is the word stages and the boundary weighting. One seed each on dev: word stages alone (`lw2`) 1.1048, gain 0.0034; boundary weight 0.25 alone (`story`) 1.1052, gain 0.0030. The three singles sum to 0.0080 against 0.0083 for the three together at 0.25 (`c1`, one seed) and 0.0091 for the claim (8 seeds). At a seed SD of 0.0012 on that dev batch the word and boundary shares cannot be separated, as the claim itself says.

Direct checks of the two signals. Boundary: on 128 dev windows holding a NUL, order 4 votes from the same story are right 62.6% of the time (22,973 votes), votes across a boundary 44.8% (5,139 votes). With frozen smoke tables and zero gates, weighting alone moves bpb from 1.5416 to 1.5363, and positions before the first NUL change by exactly 0. Word: stage 102 votes at 14.6% of positions and is right 41.4% of the time, and at 1,804 of 32,704 positions it votes where order 6 is silent. Stage 101 votes at 15,902 positions where order 6 is silent, with 38.6% of those votes right. The word stages are the least directly supported part: with zero gates on smoke tables they lose 0.0121, so their value depends on the gate, which I could not rerun at budget. A third word stage (`r1w2`) added nothing on one seed.

2. Honesty. Nothing reads targets, looks ahead or keeps state. `y` reaches only the cross-entropy calls in training. `word_hashes` scans `z` at or before the position, `run` is a cumulative count up to the position, and votes come from `tril(-1)`. On 16 dev windows with smoke parameters I changed everything after a random cut five ways: donor bytes, a NUL at the cut, all NUL, case flipped, bytes shifted so words move. Logits before the cut moved by exactly 0.0 each time. Replacing the other rows of a batch and rerunning the same batch also gave 0.0. A NUL 30 bytes earlier moves logits by a median of 1.12, so the test has power. Static rules pass: literals 2,816 of 4,096, nodes 3,596 of 8,192.

The NUL is legitimate. `_data.py` treats it as the story start, `batch(align=True)` is built on it, and byte 0 is already a softmax candidate in the incumbent. Its rate matches across data (mean gap 807 bytes in train, 800 in held-out; 64.6% of dev and 65.7% of test windows hold one; 33.2% and 34.3% of positions fall at or after the first). Windows are cut at a fixed stride, and first-NUL positions are flat across 8 bins of the window (dev 70 to 94, test 71 to 97), so the cut carries no signal. `ends` reads `x` and not the padded `z`, so the zero padding and the window edge never count as a boundary. The feature is dataset-specific but causal: any compressor sees the same byte at the same point.

Twin values are real differences, not zero: 7.6e-6 to 1.43e-5 over 16 seeds, against 0.0 on every earlier test log and tolerance 1e-4. The features do not cause them. The same code gave 0.0 on all 8 dev seeds on the agent's VM, and attempt-03, where only `mix` and `fit_tables` differ from the incumbent and eval does identical arithmetic, shows 8.6e-6 to 1.53e-5 under the same code hash. Attempt-03's eval also ran 1.283 times slower than the incumbent's at identical ticks, so this looks like a host's float noise. These features cannot defeat the twins because they have nothing ahead of the cut to hide, and a leak at the full tolerance is worth about 1.4e-4 bpb by verify.py's own note, 1.5% of the gain. The noise does take up to 14% of the tolerance on that host.

3. Dev tuning. The census of `work/r` finds 45 full-budget seed runs, all on dev, equal to `seed_runs_total` 45, with 44 before the claim was written. No tool input holds `--child`, `heldout`, `fromfile` or a `.bin` path, so the counter does not undercount here. A further 17 smoke seed runs used the default test split, zeros in the sandbox (`test_nonzero_bytes` 0), and carry nothing. There are 29 names: base, an older reference (`ppm`), a rerun of the claim, and 26 new variants, 19 before the 8-seed run and 7 after, one of those void on the parameter cap. That matches the claim's "about 20" and "six further" within one. The agent read the code and README in its copy, used Bash, Read, Monitor and ToolSearch, made no web call, and no command mentions curl, wget or a dataset. The test windows were zeroed and no test figure for the record appears in the transcript. New constants are few and round: SAMPLE, FULL, two LOCAL triples that the gate then moves, WORD_K, and the 0.9. The vote weight is a three-way pick on three different seeds (0.25 at 1.0999, 0.1 at 1.0973, 0 at 1.0990), inside noise. That is ordinary tuning, and the sealed gain shows no sign of more.

4. Price exploitation. Nothing dodges the meter. `detach()` is free, but the skipped backward truly skips `np.add.at`, so this is less work and not unpaid work. Eval is 12,180 ticks per byte, 24.4% of the 50,000 cap, against 8,923: `local_stats` adds 2,915 (of which `word_hashes` is 118, the rest two stages and the mask) and `blend` adds 342. Train Python lines 1,386,632 of 20,000,000 (incumbent 1,175,082), eval lines 113,011, item reads 0, train peak RSS 199.9 MB against 200.6 MB, parameters 4,188,406 against 4,186,846 of a 4,194,304 cap.

Wall time leans a little harder. Raw train is 112.7 s and 2.82 ns per tick against 80.8 s and 2.02, but hosts differ. Scaling the incumbent by the 1.283 above gives 103.6 s, so the entry costs 1.088 times as much per tick, and 1.055 against attempt-03's 106.8 s. Eval per tick is 4.05 ns against attempt-03's 3.94, 1.03 times. The gate's share of ticks is unchanged at 8.04%. Taking the 5.4 to 5.9 ratio for the incumbent, and assuming the entry's table phase matches attempt-03's per tick plus the 1.2% I timed on the Mac, its gate runs about 11.9 to 12.6 ns per tick, 5.9 to 6.4 times its table phase, and 34% to 36% of its wall time against 32% to 34%. That is the same known mispricing used slightly more, not a new one. Charging all 8.8% at attempt-03's marginal rate would cost about 0.0017, leaving about 0.0076, 3.8 times the margin. Seed SD is 0.00079 and 0.00071 against the incumbent's 0.00072, ratios 1.09 and 0.99 against LOW_VARIANCE_RATIO 0.3, with no flag in the verdict.

5. Selection. The gain stands clear. Stage 1 is +0.00947 +- 0.00081, p beyond margin 7.2e-12. Stage 2 is +0.00919 +- 0.00077, p 5.4e-12. The worst of 16 seeds (1.09821) beats the best incumbent seed (1.10518). Multiplying stage 1's p by all 29 names gives 2.1e-10. Both stages share the incumbent's 8 seeds, whose standard error of 0.00026 is small against the gain. The chosen single seed did regress on dev, 1.0973 to 1.0991, and the agent claimed the 8-seed figure. Claimed gain 0.0091 against sealed 0.0093 shows no shrinkage, and the dev-to-test shift matches the incumbent's (entry 1.0991 to 1.0972, incumbent 1.1082 to 1.1065). One of three claims was used.

Could not check: a per-part ablation at the real budget, so the split among schedule, word stages and boundary weight rests on attempt-03 and single-seed dev runs; the CPU behind the nonzero twins, which the logs do not name, so the host cause is inferred; the x86 split of wall time between phases, inferred from totals since this Mac shows only 1.07 to 1.17; the causality stress test on x86 or on fully trained parameters; eval RSS, which is not logged.

Verdict: real
